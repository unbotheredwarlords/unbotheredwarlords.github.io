Addon guide

Everything our addon does, page by page, as you'll find it in game.

Get the addon

Once it's installed, open it from the minimap's addon menu or type /uw. It opens on Start here: a few picks that fit your level and rank right now.

Play together

  • Guildmates near my level

    Who's online within a few levels of you, free ones first. Click a name in chat to whisper or invite.

    When
    You want company for a quest or a dungeon and don't know who's around.
    Try it
    Press Find guildmates. Chat lists up to 15 guildmates within 3 levels of you, nearest first: level, name in class colour, and zone. Anyone away, busy or inside a dungeon is greyed at the end of the list.
    Tip
    Nobody close? It says so. A line in guild chat reaches everyone, addon or not.

  • Call a group

    Writes a line in guild chat for the dungeon at your level, then you invite whoever types inv. Anyone can also just ask in guild chat.

    Members and up

    When
    You're free for a dungeon and want guildmates along.
    Try it
    Press Call a group. The tab already holds the dungeon for your level. Click once to see the line, again within 6 seconds to send it. Guild chat reads: Getting a group going for Wailing Caverns (15-24), who's in? Type inv here when you're free.
    Tip
    Everyone who types inv in the next 15 minutes is listed. Invite next invites them one per click, skipping anyone already grouped.

  • Dungeon quests

    Before a dungeon, an arrow walks you to each of its quests you can take now, then to the entrance. Pick one near your level.

    Needs Questie

    When
    Before a dungeon run. Its quests are often the best XP of the night.
    Try it
    Pick the dungeon (the one for your level comes first). Chat says how many quests are left to grab, names their givers in order, and an arrow walks the shortest way to each, then to the entrance.
    Tip
    It plans again as you go and leaves out quests you've done, already hold, or are too low for. In a group, it names who can share one you're missing.

  • Meet point

    In a group, your spot becomes the group's meet point: their markers point at you until you clear it.

    When
    Your group is spread over the zone and you want everyone in one spot.
    Try it
    In a group, press Share my spot. Each groupmate with the addon gets a line saying you set a meet point, and their marker turns toward you. Clear it when you've met.
    Tip
    A map pin you've placed is shared instead of where you stand. Groupmates see it with the addon and Questie. In a raid, the leader or an assist sets it.

  • Thank a guildmate

    Thank someone you grouped with in the last 3 hours. It counts toward their Officer path. Once a day each.

    When
    Someone carried a pull, shared a quest, or just made the run fun.
    Try it
    Target them, then press Thank my target. Chat confirms: Thanks sent to Name. It counts toward their Officer path.
    Tip
    It works for anyone you grouped with in the last 3 hours, once a day each.

Leveling helpers

Each helper has an off switch.

  • Wear clear gear upgrades

    Only when it's clear: same slot and armor type, higher item level, no stat lower. Bind on Equip and other upgrades for your class and talents ask first, and say by how much. Never in combat or a loot roll. Off: no swaps, questions or upgrade notes; upgrades stay in your bags until you put them on.

    When
    Always on while you level. Nothing to press.
    You'll see
    A clear upgrade goes on by itself, with a line in chat and in the middle of the screen: Put on [new item] (ilvl 22), was [old item] (18). Another upgrade, or a Bind on Equip piece, asks first: Upgrade? [new item]: +12% for you, more Strength and weapon damage. You wear [old item]. Put it on? When a quest lets you choose, a chat line names the best reward for you, and without Pawn a gear tooltip shows UW: +12% upgrade for you.
    Tip
    Changed your mind? Undo last swap puts the old piece back within 10 minutes, and the new one won't go on by itself again.

  • New spells onto my bars

    A new rank replaces the old one on your bars; a new spell takes the first empty slot. Never macros or items. Off: new spells and ranks wait in your spellbook.

    When
    Every time you train.
    You'll see
    Frost Armor (Rank 2) replaces the old rank on your bars. A brand-new spell lands in the first empty slot: … is on your bars.
    Tip
    No empty slot? It tells you, and the spell waits in your spellbook (P) for you to drag.

  • Space uses quest items

    When your target is part of a quest you carry an item for, and it's in range, Space uses the item. Shift+Space uses it in fights too. Off: Space only jumps.

    When
    Quests that have you use an item on something: a sample, a totem, a fallen creature.
    You'll see
    Target the quest creature, step into range, and Space uses the item instead of jumping. Out of range, Space jumps as usual.
    Tip
    Shift+Space uses the item during a fight too.

  • Delete and Make room in bags

    Delete on your bags drops the cheapest junk; with full bags, Make room swaps junk for better loot. Junk worth less than anything in full bags stays on the corpse. Shift-click Delete keeps an item. Off: no Delete or Make room buttons; you clear bags by hand.

    When
    Your bags are full, mid-quest, far from a vendor.
    You'll see
    A Delete button above your bags names your cheapest junk; hover for its value. With full bags at a corpse, the loot window offers Make room to drop junk for the better item. At a vendor, a Sell button gathers items worth more sold than disenchanted.
    Tip
    A delete worth 1 gold or more asks first. Shift-click Delete to keep an item for good.

  • Quest guide marker

    The map pin and on-screen marker point at your tracked quest's next step. Wandering NPCs: along their route, and a line once in sight. Your own pin always wins. Off: no map pin or marker for your next step.

    Needs Questie

    When
    Questing, when you'd rather not open the map every minute.
    You'll see
    Track a quest and an on-screen marker points at its next step, with a pin on the map.
    Tip
    Your own map pin always wins: the marker steps aside until you turn it back on.

  • Quest chain tags

    Questie's tracker shows each quest's step in its chain (3/7); hover for every step. Off: Questie's tracker shows quest names only.

    Needs Questie

    When
    A quest that leads somewhere bigger, and you want to know how far along you are.
    You'll see
    Beside a quest in Questie's tracker, its step in the chain in grey, like 3/7. List my chains prints every chain in your log in chat.
    Tip
    Hover a quest title in the tracker for every step of its chain.

  • Keep the quest window open

    After a turn-in, DialogueUI waits a moment for the next quest. Off: it closes as it normally would.

    Needs DialogueUI

    When
    Quest hubs where one turn-in opens the next quest.
    You'll see
    After a turn-in, DialogueUI stays open about 4 seconds, so the follow-up shows without talking to the NPC again.
    Tip
    If the next quest or the NPC's menu appears, it simply carries on.

  • Send mats to my alts

    One click mails crafting materials to your own alt whose professions use them. Off: no Send mats button; mail by hand.

    When
    At a mailbox, with herbs, ore or cloth one of your alts can use.
    You'll see
    A button like Send mats to Name (3) next to the mailbox. One click mails them. Hover first to see what goes.
    Tip
    Log in to each alt once with the addon on so it knows them. What would go lists it ahead of time, and anything worth less than the postage stays.

  • Dungeon lines in chat

    At a level where a dungeon opens, one line in your chat names it. Inside one with a group, a line names its quests and who carries each, and a button tells the group. Off: neither line, and no button.

    When
    Each time you level, and on the way into a dungeon with a group.
    You'll see
    At a level where a dungeon opens, one line: Level 15: Ruins of Lordaeron and Wailing Caverns are in range now. Ask guild chat for a group. Inside: Wailing Caverns quests in your group: Deviate Hides (you, Ana Brook).
    Tip
    Tell the group this run's quests above chat posts the list in party chat, so groupmates without the addon see it too.

Guild

  • Welcome window

    Our norms and how to get started.

    When
    Your first day, or any time you want the norms again.
    Try it
    Press Open it. The window shows the helpers already at work for you, our norms, and who to ask for help.

  • Crafting

    Guildmates see what you can craft, and you see theirs. Open each profession window once to add recipes. Off: nothing is shared, and online guildmates forget what they had.

    When
    You need something made, or want guildmates to know what you can make.
    Try it
    Press Who crafts what. Chat shows your own professions with how many recipes are shared, and how many guildmates are in the directory.
    Tip
    Open each profession window once so its recipes are added. Training a new one? Open its window once more.

  • Rallies and group nights

    A login line and a heads-up 10 minutes before each Rally or group night. Off: no login line or 10-minute heads-ups.

    When
    You'd like to know when a Rally or group night is on without checking Discord.
    Try it
    Press What's on for the list, like Rally today 20:00 (6 going). At login you get one line for the next day, and a heads-up 10 minutes before each event starts.
    Tip
    Event details are on each event's Discord card.

  • Loot rules

    Main spec before off spec, with rolls only. /roll for main spec, /roll 99 for off spec.

    When
    Any time a drop is rolled for in a guild group.
    Try it
    Press How rolls work. In game, type /roll for main spec or /roll 99 for off spec. When a roll starts in a guild group, chat reminds you: Need only for your main spec, Greed for off spec, alts and selling.
    Tip
    When a leader runs the roll, main spec goes first, then regular raiders, then whoever has fewer main-spec wins this week, then the highest roll.

  • Guild jobs

    Who holds each guild job, read from guild notes, and how to take one on.

    When
    You'd like to help the guild in a small, steady way.
    Try it
    Press Show jobs. Chat lists Welcome buddy, Recruiter and Event host, who holds each, and how many more each wants.
    Tip
    Want one? Whisper an officer and they'll add it to your guild note.

  • What you like

    Pick what you enjoy: leveling, dungeons, raids, PvP, professions, social, exploring, RP. Linked on Discord, these show as your roles there.

    When
    Right after you join, so guildmates who enjoy the same things can find you.
    Try it
    Press Pick. Tick any of eight tiles: leveling, dungeons, raids, PvP, professions, social, exploring, RP. Then Save.
    Tip
    Change them whenever you like. Linked on Discord, they become your roles there.

  • Rate a leader

    After a guild group's boss kill, one private ask, at most once a day. Off: no asks.

    When
    After a guild dungeon or raid leader takes your group through a boss.
    Try it
    Once the group ends and you're out of combat, you may get one private ask. Give a score from 1 to 5, and if you like any of: trust, care, integrity, leadership, patience.
    Tip
    Your rating is private: it's only ever added into totals from 5 or more guildmates. At most one ask a day.

  • Contact the Guild Master

    The Guild Master's BattleTag, for anything you'd rather not post in guild chat.

    When
    Something you'd rather not post in guild chat.
    Try it
    Press Show. Chat gives the Guild Master's BattleTag to add as a Battle.net friend.

  • Discord

    Optional. The invite link, ready to copy. Needed only for raid signups and raid nights. To link this character there, type /link, press Enter, then your character's name in the box.

    When
    You'd like to raid with us, or just hang out in voice.
    Try it
    Press Copy invite. A small box opens with the link selected. Press Ctrl+C (Cmd+C on a Mac) and paste it into your browser.

Your addons

  • Addon kit

    The few addons the guild plays with. Shows what's missing or doubled up; Fix turns duplicates off and kit addons on.

    When
    After you install, after a break, or when something looks off.
    Try it
    Press Check my kit. Chat lists what's missing and what's doubled up. Everyone's kit is EllesmereUI and Questie; raiders add Deadly Boss Mods or BigWigs. Fix my kit turns the duplicates off and the kit on. Optional extras lists sixteen more, each with what it's for.
    Tip
    Fix never installs anything; it names what's still to add.

  • Guild layout

    Imports the raid frames, bars and meter the officers shared, through each addon's own import.

    Needs EllesmereUI

    When
    You'd like raid frames, bars and a meter that match the group's.
    Try it
    Press Use it. Each addon's own import opens in turn, and your current layout stays saved. DialogueUI look does the same for quest windows.
    Tip
    Do it out of combat. An addon you don't have switched on is skipped.

  • Class macros

    The guild's tested macros for your class, added in one click. Your own macro slots are left free.

    When
    You want macros other players of your class trust, without writing your own.
    Try it
    Press Show mine. Chat lists your class's guild macros, best first, each with when it helps. Adding one puts it on your cursor; click an action bar slot to place it.
    Tip
    A one-click add leaves some of your macro slots free for your own.

  • Something broken?

    A short report for the officers, a self-test of UW Guild, and your version against the newest.

    When
    Something errors, or an addon stops working.
    Try it
    Press Report. A box opens with a one-line report already selected: copy it and paste it where the box says. Self-test checks the addon and prints a short result. Version tells you whether a newer one is out.

  • Fishing sound

    Music and ambience off, effects up, sound on in the background, so you hear the splash. Again to undo.

    When
    A long fishing session where you keep missing the splash.
    Try it
    Press Fishing sound: music and ambience go off, effects go up, and sound keeps playing in the background. Press it again to undo.
    Tip
    Forgot to switch it back? Your sound settings return at your next login.

  • All switches

    Every automatic helper with its on and off, in the game's own Settings window. Turning one off never changes your rank or progress.

    When
    You'd like one helper on and another off.
    Try it
    Press Open settings, or go to Esc, Options, AddOns, UW Guild. Each helper has a tick box and a line on what you lose without it.

Raids

  • Raid plan

    Your own job in each step of the raid leader's plan. The leader pastes the plan code; you see your lines.

    When
    Raid night, before a boss.
    Try it
    When the raid leader sends the plan, press Show my plan. You see the boss's name and only your own numbered steps. If the plan doesn't name you, it says so: ask the leader where they want you.

  • Raid records

    Guild kills, attendance and loot awards, shared live from the raid. Want to raid? Tell any officer.

    When
    Nothing to do: it runs during guild raids.
    You'll see
    Kills, who attended and who won what are shared with the guild as they happen, even when the Guild Master isn't in the raid.
    Tip
    Awards show in the loot log in our Discord.

Officers see one more page in the same window, with their tools for leading groups.
