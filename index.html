However you play

Find your place by the fire

  • 🔥

    New here, or back after years

    A levelling buddy in your first few days and an officer to ask anything. No question is too small.

  • 🗡️

    Dungeon crews

    Post /lfg in Discord and the right people get a ping. Your group gets its own voice room.

  • 🛡️

    Raiders

    Hyjal Summit (20) and Barrow Deeps (10) from 9 Dec. Main spec before off spec, every award logged, no GDKP.

  • 🍺

    Chill and social

    Campfires in game, hangouts in voice, and matches with people who play when you do. Real life comes first.

Three steps

From stranger to guildie

  1. Join the Discord

    The invite opens in #welcome-rules. Give the short guild norms a read.

  2. Click Get started

    Link your character, or pick Apply to join if you're not in the guild yet. An officer sends your in-game invite within 48 hours.

  3. Meet your people

    Say hi, meet your buddy, hop into voice. You're one of us now. Stay unbothered.
