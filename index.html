However you play

Find your place by the fire

  • 🔥

    New here, or back after years

    Ask guild chat anything. No question is too small. New to WoW? Say so in our Discord and we'll look for a guildmate to buddy you.

  • 🍺

    Chill and social

    Campfires in game, hangouts in voice, and matches with people who play when you do. Real life comes first.

  • 🗡️

    Dungeon crews

    Anyone can start one, no rank needed. In game, type /g anyone up for WC? type inv and invite who answers. In Discord, /lfg pings the right people.

  • 🛡️

    Raids, now and then

    Raid groups when enough of us want one. Main spec before off spec, every award logged, no GDKP.

Two ways in

From stranger to guildie

In game

  1. Ask any Unbothered Warlords member in game for an invite.
  2. Once you're in, ask guild chat for anything: groups, help, crafts. See someone ask? Reply or invite them.
  3. Optional: the free UW Guild addon shows guildmates online near your level, all in game.

On Discord

  1. Join the Discord. The invite opens in #welcome-rules; give the short guild norms a read.
  2. In Get started, click Link my character, or Apply to join if you're not in the guild yet. An officer sends your in-game invite within 48 hours.
  3. Say hi, find a group, hop into voice.

You're one of us now. Stay unbothered. No drama in public, fair open loot, all ages and levels welcome.
