Ranks and the addon

How moving up works here, and what our addon does.

Ranks

  • 🌱

    Trial

    Everyone starts here.

  • 🔥

    Member

    Seven days in the guild and one group with guildmates. Then an officer promotes you.

  • ⚔️

    Raider

    Want to raid? Tell any officer and you're a Raider. Nobody has to. Before we raid together, Raiders join our Discord and link their character.

  • 🛡️

    Officer

    For people who help guildmates or lead groups well. After 30 days in the guild, it's a chat with the Guild Master. No raiding needed.

Been here a week and grouped with guildmates? Whisper any officer in game for your promotion.

Invited by an officer as a friend? They can make you a Member right away.

What the addon does

UW Guild is our free addon.

  • 👥

    Guildmates near your level

    Shows who's online within a few levels of you.

  • 📣

    Call a group

    Once you're a Member, one click asks guild chat for the dungeon at your level.

  • 🧭

    Help while you level

    Tells you when a dungeon opens at your level, marks your quest's next step and puts on clear gear upgrades. Each helper has an off switch.

  • 🍺

    Crafting and thanks

    See what guildmates can craft. Thank someone you grouped with.

Get the addon

One click on Windows. Mac and Linux steps are on the same page.
