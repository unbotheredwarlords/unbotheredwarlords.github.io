Ranks

How moving up works here.

  1. Trial

    Everyone starts here.

  2. Member

    Seven days in the guild and one group with guildmates. Then an officer promotes you.

    • Raider

      Want to raid? Tell any officer and you're a Raider. Nobody has to. Before we raid together, Raiders join our Discord and link their character.

    • Officer

      For people who help guildmates or lead groups well. After 30 days in the guild, it's a chat with the Guild Master. No raiding needed.

Been here a week and grouped with guildmates? Whisper any officer in game for your promotion.

Invited by an officer? They can make you a Member right away.
